How to Play

👆

Fly

Drag anywhere on the screen — your ship follows your finger's movement. Keyboard: arrows or WASD.

🔥

Shoot

Auto-fire is on by default. Turn it off in settings to fire only while touching (or holding Space).

🚀

Missiles

Tap the missile button — or tap with a second finger — to launch a homing missile. Keyboard: X.

🍗

Drumsticks

Every roasted chicken may drop food. Collect 100 drumsticks for an extra life.

🎁

Gifts

Weapon swaps your gun, + adds power, Missiles restocks, Shield absorbs one hit, Life is a spare ship.

🥚

Watch out

Eggs, chickens, asteroids and boss attacks all hurt. Losing a ship also costs two weapon levels.

👑

Bosses

Every fifth wave a boss hen arrives with three escalating phases. Break its health bar to feast.
