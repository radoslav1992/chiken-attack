Controls

Mouse and keyboard — click the game to capture the mouse · W A S D to move · mouse to turn · left click or F to fire · E or Space to open doors · 1–7 or the wheel to change weapon · Tab for the map · Esc for the menu.

Keyboard only — arrow keys move and turn, F fires, Shift walks.

Touch — left stick to move, drag the right side to turn, FIRE and USE under your thumb. A gamepad works too.
