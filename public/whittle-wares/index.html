How it works
  • Morning. Drag anywhere to walk. Stand on a plant to gather it. Walking and gathering both cost stamina.
  • Home. Walk back to the door when you are done — or run out of stamina and be carried.
  • Evening. Set a price for everything on the shelf, then open up. Two goods are wanted each day and one is not.
  • Your journal. Complete seasonal goals, collect maker badges and earn guild stamps. Seasons change every 10 game days.
  • A lasting shop. Pay rent every fifth day. After day 30, continue into a career with 20 guild upgrades and three advanced recipes. Rent stops growing.
  • Take a break. Your place, forest and shop save as you play on this browser. The first 30 days determine your leaderboard score.
