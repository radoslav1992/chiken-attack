How it works
  • Morning. Drag anywhere to walk. Stand on a plant to gather it. Walking and gathering both cost stamina.
  • Home. Walk back to the door when you are done — or run out of stamina and be carried.
  • Evening. Set a price for everything on the shelf, then open up. Two goods are wanted each day and one is not.
  • The fifth day. Rent, and it grows. Standing still is losing slowly.
