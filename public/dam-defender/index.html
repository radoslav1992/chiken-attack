A fresh challenge

How to hold the river
  1. Select a numbered clearing and build a tower. Spend wood between waves.
  2. Acorns and watchtowers hit flying foes. Logs and brambles control ground raiders.
  3. Open the floodgates when enemies cluster. Each flood costs 40 water; below 25, your dam takes more damage.
  4. Choose a woodland perk every three waves. Beat the boss at wave 12, or keep going in endless mode.
